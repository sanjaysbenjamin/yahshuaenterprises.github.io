How we work

From idea to working system.

Each project follows the same five stages, and you approve the scope, cost and design before any development starts.

  1. Discover

    We learn your goals, users, budget and deadline.

  2. Design

    You review screens, features and a fixed quote.

  3. Build

    We develop in short cycles and demo progress regularly.

  4. Deploy

    The system goes live, devices are installed, staff are trained.

  5. Support

    We monitor, update and fix issues under a support plan.
